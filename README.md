# Privacy Policy ? Yale Delala

**Effective date:** 10 November 2026
**Last updated:** 10 November 2026

Yale Delala is a marketplace app for buying, selling and renting in Ethiopia.
This policy explains what we collect when you use the app, why we collect it,
who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do with it.

The app is operated by Elilita Solution ("Yale Delala", "we", "us"), of
Addis Ababa, Ethiopia. If you have a question about this policy, write
to support@yaledelala.com.

By using the app you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not
use the app.

---

## 1. Information you give us

**When you create an account.** Your first name and father's name, your phone
number, and your password. An email address is optional; if you give one, we
use it to verify your account and to send password resets. We send a one-time
code by SMS to confirm that the phone number is yours.

**When you post an ad.** The title, description, price, category and location
you enter, and the photos you choose from your device. Everything in an ad is
published to other users ? see section 3.

**When you pay.** Posting fees and Featured upgrades are paid inside the app.
We collect the mobile wallet number you enter, the order reference and whether
the payment succeeded. We never see or store your wallet PIN, your full card
number or your bank credentials; those go directly to the payment provider.

**When you contact us.** Anything you choose to write to our support address.

## 2. Information we collect as you use the app

**Your activity.** The ads you save to Favourites, the ads you have recently
viewed, and counts of views and leads on the ads you post. Sellers see totals
for their own ads; they do not see who viewed them.

**Location.** The app asks you to choose a city or area for an ad and for
filtering results. It does not read your device's GPS location, and it does not
request a location permission.

**Technical information.** Our servers record standard request information such
as your IP address, the time of the request, the app version and the language
you have chosen. The app stores a login token on your device so that you stay
signed in.

The app does not include advertising, and it does not track you across other
apps or websites.

## 3. What is public

Yale Delala is a public marketplace. An ad you publish ? its photos, title,
description, price, category and location, together with your display name and
the contact options you enable ? is visible to anyone using the app or the
website. Do not put anything in an ad that you do not want to be public,
including identity documents, bank details or your home address.

When a buyer uses the call or message button on your ad, your phone number is
shown to that buyer so they can reach you.

## 4. Why we use your information

| Purpose | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| Running your account | Signing you in, verifying your phone number, password resets |
| Publishing your ads | Showing them in search, in categories and on your profile |
| Connecting buyers and sellers | Call, message and the seller profile on each ad |
| Taking payment | Posting fees and Featured upgrades, and your order history |
| Moderation and safety | Reviewing ads in moderated categories, acting on reports, stopping fraud and abuse |
| Support | Answering you when you contact us |
| Improving the app | Understanding which parts of the app are used, and fixing faults |
| Legal obligations | Keeping records we are required to keep, and responding to lawful requests |

## 5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information.

We share it only with:

- **Payment providers**, to process a payment you have started. They handle
  your wallet, bank or card credentials under their own privacy policies.
- **Service providers** who host our servers, deliver our SMS messages and send
  our email, acting on our instructions.
- **Other users**, but only the information described in section 3.
- **Authorities**, where we are required by Ethiopian law to disclose
  information, or where it is necessary to protect someone's safety or our
  legal rights.

## 6. How long we keep it

We keep your account information for as long as your account exists. Ads and
their photos are kept while they are live and for a period afterwards so that
you can renew or repost them. Payment records are kept for as long as
accounting and tax rules require. When you delete your account we remove or
anonymise your information, except what we must keep for legal, accounting or
fraud-prevention reasons.

## 7. Your choices

- **See and correct your information.** Your account details and your ads can
  be viewed and edited in the app, under Account.
- **Delete an ad.** Use Manage ad ? Delete on any of your ads.
- **Delete your account.** Request deletion at https://yaledelala.com/account/delete, or write to
  support@yaledelala.com from the phone number or email address on the account. We
  will confirm and complete the deletion within 30 days.
- **Change your language.** Account ? Language, between English and Amharic.
- **Object or complain.** Write to support@yaledelala.com. You may also complain to the
  relevant Ethiopian data protection authority.

## 8. Children

Yale Delala is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect
information from children. If you believe a child has given us their
information, write to support@yaledelala.com and we will delete it.

## 9. Security

We protect data in transit with HTTPS, store your password only in hashed form,
and keep your login token in your device's secure storage. Access to our systems
is limited to staff who need it. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a
strong password, do not reuse it elsewhere, and sign out on devices you share.

Please also follow the safety advice shown on every ad: meet in a public place,
check the item before you pay, and never send money in advance.

## 10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version at this address and update
the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we use your
information, we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
